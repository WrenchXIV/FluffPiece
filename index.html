01

About this guide

This document is the source of truth for how the FluffPiece logo is used across every surface — broadcast bumpers, app icons, board books, plush hangtags, T-shirt tags, and beyond. It exists so the show feels like itself everywhere a child meets it.

The audience is small and sharp. Two-year-olds recognize shapes before words; eight-year-olds recognize knockoffs instantly. The rules below protect the friendliness of the mark while keeping it crisp at icon size and safe on every background a co-branded partner might throw at it.

  • Always keep the two-tone color relationship — the lighter "Fluff" leads, the deeper "Piece" follows.
  • Always use an approved master file. Never re-letter or reconstruct the mark.
  • Choose the sticker variant whenever the background is busy, photographic, or close in tone to the brand greens.
02

Primary logo

The stacked two-tone wordmark is the master mark. Letters sit in two greens with a soft drop shadow that gives them their three-dimensional, plushy presence. The mark is always used as a single locked artwork — never typeset, never rebuilt.

Use the wordmark editor below to preview the four approved master files (one-line / two-line × halo off / halo on) on any background, fine-tune the drop shadow, and download a PNG of the current composite for layout comping.

03

Anatomy

The mark is built from four components. Each plays a role; removing one breaks the others.

04

Lockups

Three approved lockups cover every situation. Pick by surface shape and background — not by personal preference.

05

Color palette

Two greens carry the brand. Cream and ink ground it. The accents are reserved for moments — a pop in a title card, a sticker on a board book — never as primary color. Click any swatch to retune it.

06

Color variations

In order of preference. Reach for the next variation only when the one above it cannot reproduce faithfully.

07

Clear space

Reserve a zone of empty space around the mark equal to 1× — where x equals the height of the lowercase "u" in Fluff. No type, no graphics, no edges intrude inside this boundary.

1×
08

Minimum size

Below these sizes the bubble forms thicken into mush and the smile baseline reads as crooked. Don't.

09

Approved backgrounds

The mark is happiest on flat brand colors and clean photography. Avoid placing it on its own primary green, on busy patterns, or on photography that competes for attention with the wordmark. Switch to the sticker variant the moment any of those conditions can't be controlled.

10

Misuse

Each of these breaks the mark in a way kids will feel before they can name. Don't ship any of them.
